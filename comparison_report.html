📌 The Core Finding

Both scenarios start with the same property, the same sale price (€315,000), and the same Spanish tax bill. The only difference is the ownership structure.

In Scenario A (personal), the Foreign Tax Credit Relief mechanism works perfectly — the Spanish tax paid almost completely cancels out the UK tax bill. Philip pays one layer of tax and keeps £243,358.

In Scenario B (company), the FTCR does work at the corporate level — but the money must then be extracted through two further tax events (Corporation Tax top-up + personal CGT on MVL) that would never have existed in a personal ownership structure. Philip ultimately keeps £183,264.

Wincham's claimed "saving" of approximately £10,702 in Spanish purchase taxes (ITP) in 2019 has been comprehensively wiped out — resulting in a net financial harm to Philip of approximately £49,392 after accounting for that upfront saving.

The effective tax rate on the personally-held property was 10.9%. The effective rate through the company structure was 32.9% — three times higher.
